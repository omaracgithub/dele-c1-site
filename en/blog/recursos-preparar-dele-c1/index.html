Best resources to prepare for the DELE C1

Updated: June 2026

Preparing for the DELE C1 requires a combination of materials: textbooks for the exam structure, apps for daily practice, podcasts for listening comprehension, and sources of model exams. Here we present the most useful resources organized by category.

Preparation Books

Specific manuals for the DELE C1

  • El Cronómetro C1 (Edinumen): Excellent for practicing with timing. Includes specific strategies for each task and complete timed exams. Ideal for the last months of preparation.
  • Preparación al DELE C1 (Edelsa): Classic manual with complete exam models and detailed explanations of each test. Includes audio CD for listening comprehension.
  • Las claves del DELE C1 (Difusión): Practical approach with tips, solved models, and progressive preparation activities.
  • Objetivo DELE C1 (SGEL): Includes practice exams with commented solutions and audio transcripts.

Advanced Grammar and Vocabulary

  • Gramática de uso del español C1-C2 (SM): Grammatical reference with practical exercises for advanced levels.
  • Uso interactivo del vocabulario B2-C1 (Edelsa): Vocabulary organized by thematic fields with contextualized exercises.
  • Competencia gramatical en uso C1 (Edelsa): Advanced grammar exercises with clear explanations.

Mobile Applications

DELE C1 Prep (our app)

Designed specifically for preparing for the DELE C1, our application offers:

  • Reading comprehension exercises with authentic texts
  • Listening comprehension practice with varied audios
  • Language use exercises (grammar and vocabulary in context)
  • Progress tracking and statistics
  • Daily practice tailored to your level

Other complementary apps

  • Anki: To create vocabulary cards with spaced repetition. Look for shared decks of "DELE C1 vocabulary."
  • SpanishDict: Comprehensive dictionary with conjugations, examples, and pronunciation.
  • Radio Ambulante (podcast app): Stories in Spanish from all over Latin America, excellent for listening comprehension.

Recommended Podcasts

Listening to podcasts regularly improves your listening comprehension and exposes you to natural vocabulary:

For comprehension practice

  • Hoy en El País: Daily news from the newspaper El País. Formal register, current topics.
  • Radio Ambulante (NPR): Non-fiction stories from all over Latin America. Variety of accents and topics.
  • A vivir que son dos días (Cadena SER): Current affairs magazine with interviews and debates.
  • Aprendemos juntos (BBVA): Lectures and interviews on education, science, and culture.

For specialized vocabulary

  • Economía para quedarte sin amigos: Economics explained in an accessible way.
  • Ciencia Brújula (RNE): Scientific dissemination in Spanish.
  • Carne Cruda: Independent journalism on politics and society.

YouTube Channels

  • Instituto Cervantes (official channel): Conferences, presentations, and cultural events.
  • RTVE Noticias: Complete news broadcasts to practice news comprehension.
  • DW Español: International news in clear and well-articulated Spanish.
  • TED en Español: Talks on various topics with available subtitles.
  • Documentales de La 2: Quality documentaries on science, history, and culture.

Sources of Practice Exams

Official Models

  • Web of Instituto Cervantes: Publishes free exam models with audios and solutions. It is the most reliable resource because it reflects exactly the real format.
  • Exams from previous convocations: Some exam centers publish models from previous years.

Complementary Material

  • Our exam model section: Available at c1.prepdele.com/modelo-examen
  • Books with complete exams: El Cronómetro and Preparación al DELE include several models.

Press and Reading

To improve your reading comprehension and expand vocabulary:

  • El País, El Mundo, La Vanguardia: Spanish generalist press
  • BBC Mundo: International news in neutral Spanish
  • Revista Muy Interesante: Scientific dissemination
  • Letras Libres: Essay and culture
  • Jot Down: Narrative and cultural journalism

How to Combine Resources

An effective daily routine could be:

  1. Morning (20 min): Exercises in the DELE C1 Prep app (reading or listening)
  2. Transport/walk (30 min): Podcast in Spanish
  3. Afternoon (30 min): Exercises from the preparation book
  4. Evening (15 min): Reading the press + noting new vocabulary
